About Sasah AI

We make AI useful for everyday work.

Sasah AI is an Indian AI company. We build custom AI solutions for businesses and ready-to-use products for workplaces and homes.

What we believe

  • Useful beats impressive. AI should save real hours or real money, or it isn't worth building.
  • Data belongs to its owner. Wherever possible, our systems can run at the customer's own site.
  • Honesty sells better. No fake logos, no made-up accuracy numbers. Pilot first, then decide.
  • Built for India. WhatsApp, Hindi, Tally, PF and ESI are first-class, not afterthoughts.

FAQ

Common questions

Do we need a lot of data to start?

No. Many projects start with what you already have: PDFs, Excel sheets, past chats or camera feeds. In the discovery call we check what is usable.

Which AI models do you use?

Whatever fits the job and budget: cloud models from OpenAI, Anthropic or Google, or open models that run locally on your own server.

Can it run without sending our data outside?

Yes, if you need that. We can run local models on your hardware. Cloud models are cheaper to start with; we explain the trade-off before you decide.

Will the AI make up numbers?

We design against it. Figures like attendance, salary or stock come from your own system; the AI only finds and explains them. Answers show their sources where possible.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. After the discovery call we share a written plan with milestones, starting with a small proof of concept.

What does it cost to run every month?

Two parts: our support fee (if you choose it) and AI usage, which the model provider bills at actual rates. Local models have no per-message cost but need hardware.

Who owns the work?

Ownership and licensing are written into the agreement before we start, so there are no surprises.

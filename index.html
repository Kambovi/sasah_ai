FAQ

Common questions

What does Sasah AI do?

We build custom AI solutions for businesses (Sasah Forge) and sell ready-to-use products: face-recognition attendance with HR & payroll (Sasah Workforce), face recognition for home CCTV (Sasah HomeWatch) and websites (Sasah Studio).

Why is custom AI your flagship?

Every business has a different problem. Our ready products prove we can ship working AI; Forge is where we build the exact thing your business needs.

Will our data leave our premises?

Only if you choose so. Our attendance and home products can run on a server at your site, and custom AI can use local models on your own hardware. We explain the options before you decide.

Do you work outside your city?

Software, AI and website projects can be done remotely across India. Camera installations are planned per location, so tell us where your site is.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need: the number of sites, cameras and people, or the scope of your project. Tell us a few details and we'll send a clear written quote by email before any work starts. You can compare what each plan includes on our plans page.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. We are happy to sign an NDA before you share business data or documents.

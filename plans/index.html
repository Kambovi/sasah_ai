FAQ

Questions about plans and quotes

Why don't you show prices on the website?

Every site is different: the number of cameras, gates, people and the hardware you already have all change the cost. A written quote based on your setup is fairer than a generic price list.

How quickly will I get a quote?

Usually within 1–2 working days of receiving your details. For camera products we may ask a few questions or do a quick site or CCTV check first.

What is “recognised by name”?

People you add with a photo: family, domestic help, employees. The camera tells you who they are. Unknown faces are detected and alerted without any limit, and never count against a plan.

Are taxes included in the quote?

Your quote shows GST separately, so you know exactly what you will pay.

What is usually quoted separately?

New cameras or server hardware (at actual cost), WhatsApp / AI provider charges where used (at actual cost), and the domain for a website (registered in your name).

Why per camera and not per employee?

So a growing company doesn't pay more each month for every new worker. One camera covers one gate and up to 200 people recognised by name.

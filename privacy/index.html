Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026.

1. Who we are

Sasah AI (“we”, “us”) builds custom AI solutions and the products Sasah Workforce, Sasah HomeWatch and Sasah Studio. Contact: our email.

2. Data we collect on this website

  • Enquiry form: name, company, email, phone (if you choose to share it), product of interest, message and any optional fields you fill in.
  • Email: if you write to us, we keep your email address and messages to reply to you.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends (such as IP address) may be logged by our hosting provider for security.

We do not use advertising trackers or cookies for advertising on this website.

3. How we use it

Only to reply to your enquiry, share demos and quotes, and keep a record of our conversation. We do not sell your data.

4. Data inside our products

For Sasah Workforce and Sasah HomeWatch, face images, face templates and attendance or visitor records are processed for our customer. The customer decides what is collected and is the Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023; we act as their processor. On-premise installs store this data on the customer's own hardware. We access it only when the customer asks for support.

5. Consent and deletion

People are enrolled only after consent. When a person leaves or withdraws consent, their face data is deleted and a deletion certificate can be issued. To delete your website enquiry data, email us.

6. Sharing

We use a small number of service providers to run this website and receive enquiries (for example our hosting provider and form service). They process data only on our instructions.

7. Retention

Enquiry data is kept as long as needed to respond and for reasonable business records, then deleted.

8. Your rights and grievances

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or raise a grievance, by writing to our email. We aim to respond within 30 days.

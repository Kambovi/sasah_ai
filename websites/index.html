FAQ

Common questions

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, and you get the files.

What will hosting cost?

Simple business sites can often run on free plans of Netlify or Cloudflare Pages. You pay for the domain yearly. Sites with a backend are quoted with hosting.

Do I need to write the content?

No. We write the copy for you in English (or Hindi) after a short call. You review and approve.

Can I edit it later?

Yes. We can hand over simple editing, or make changes for you under a small monthly plan.

How long does it take?

It depends on the number of pages and how quickly content and photos are ready. You get a timeline with the quote.

Can you add an AI chatbot?

Yes. Through Sasah Forge we can add a chatbot that answers from your own information and sends leads to your email or WhatsApp.
